FAQ

Questions contractors ask

I don't have an existing profile. Can you still help?

Yes. Send whatever you have — a project list, CVs, certificates, photos — and the call fills in the rest.

Is a company profile compulsory for tenders?

Not always, but many tenders ask for one or score the information it contains under functionality. A clear profile makes that evidence easy to award.

Do you submit tenders for us?

No. You stay in control of every submission. We prepare the profile and supporting documents, and on Bid Support we check your pack against the tender's returnables before you submit.

Who owns the files?

You do. You receive the final print-ready PDF, and the profile is yours to use in every tender. Within 3 months we update it once for free (a new project, a new staff member, a renewed certificate); after that, updates are quoted per change, or included on Bid Support.

How do I pay?

By EFT. The Profile and Tender Pack are paid upfront; Bid Support is billed monthly.

Which industries do you work with?

Contractors and service companies that tender: civil and building contractors, electrical, mechanical, security, facilities, engineering and industrial suppliers.
